# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in DevSheets deserves a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience. This applies to contributors, maintainers, and visitors in project spaces such as issues, pull requests, and discussions, as well as when representing the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect regardless of their identity, background, or experience.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work and remain courteous during disagreements.
- Respect personal boundaries and ask permission before sharing someone else's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, or sexualized insults and unwanted sexual attention.
- Sharing private information without permission or retaliating against someone for reporting a concern.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the repository owner privately using a contact method listed on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/RaviTejaLadi), if available. Include what happened, where and when it occurred, and any relevant links or screenshots you can safely share. For abusive content or behavior on GitHub, you can also use GitHub's reporting tools. Keep sensitive details out of public issues and pull requests.

Maintainers will review reports and limit disclosure to what is necessary to investigate and respond. Depending on the severity and whether the behavior recurs, maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban someone from project spaces.
